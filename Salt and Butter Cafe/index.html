Welcome

Your neighbourhood café, with a waterfront view.

Salt and Butter is an easy-going café by the river in Ermington. Come for a coffee, stay for breakfast, and take your time.

It's a place to meet a friend, bring the family or sit outside on your own with something good to eat. We're open every day from 7:30 in the morning.

Our cafe

Made for slow mornings, quick coffees & everything between.

Some people are in and out with a takeaway on the way to work. Others settle in for a long breakfast. Both are fine by us.

The kitchen does breakfast, brunch, burgers and toasties, and there's usually something sweet in the cabinet. Sit inside or out, catch up with someone, or just have a quiet one by the water.
