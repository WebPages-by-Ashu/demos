the locals

our patch of Agnes Water

Ten acres of bush, a mob of kangaroos, and room to slow down

Horizons is a small campground in a bush setting on 10 acres at Agnes Water, about seven minutes' drive from town.

There are powered and unpowered sites, hot showers, a well-equipped camp kitchen and a fire pit. The views are big, the sunsets are worth being back at camp for, and there's wildlife and birdlife everywhere you look.

Wander the pathways through the gardens during the day, then come up for the kangaroo feeding, held every afternoon. It's quiet, peaceful and relaxed out here.

— Denise & everyone at Horizons

pick your spot

Two kinds of site

Tents, rooftop tents, campervans, motorhomes, off-road camper trailers and caravans up to 19 feet are all welcome. Short stays and longer ones too.

keep it simple

Unpowered sites

Pitch the tent or park the camper, set the chairs out and take in the view. The showers and camp kitchen are a short walk away.

plug in

Powered sites

Power for the van, camper or motorhome. Fill your water tank before you set up, as there's no water hook-up at the sites.

For rates and availability, ring the office on (07) 4974 7783.

what's here

Around camp

  • Hot showers & toilets

    A clean amenities block with hot showers and flushing toilets.

  • Camp kitchen

    Well equipped, with a stove, microwave, kettle, pots and utensils.

  • Fire pit

    One communal pit near the kitchen, with the wood supplied.

  • Verandah & deck

    A big verandah and deck with views out over the treetops. The spot for sunset.

  • Kangaroo feeding

    Every afternoon. Come along and meet the mob.

  • Birdlife

    Peacocks, brush turkeys, kookaburras and cockatoos. Bring the binoculars.

  • Garden paths

    Pathways wind through the gardens around camp. Good for a wander.

  • Drinking water

    Drinking water on site. Tanks are fill-only, with no hook-up at the sites.

Good to know

  • Bookings are by phone. Ring the office on (07) 4974 7783.
  • No pets, sorry. This is a wildlife sanctuary.
  • Caravans up to 19 feet. The driveway is steep, so big rigs won't make it in.
  • Fires in the communal fire pit only.
  • Water: fill your tank on the way in. There's no hook-up at the sites.
  • Best for adults and teenagers. It's not really set up for young children.

meet the mob

A sanctuary first

Horizons is a wildlife sanctuary as well as a campground. We're wildlife carers, and have spent many years raising orphaned and injured kangaroos and releasing them back to the bush.

The mob roams free across the property, and every afternoon they come in for a feed. Campers are welcome to come along. All camp fees go towards the work of the sanctuary.

getting your bearings

Where we are

Agnes Water is a laid-back coastal town between Bundaberg and Gladstone, at the southern end of the Great Barrier Reef. We're tucked into the bush about seven minutes' drive from the main street.

  1. Horizons

    That's us: 15 Fitzroy Crescent. Punch it into the sat nav.

  2. Agnes Water

    Supermarkets, chemist, doctors, a tavern and plenty of cafés and restaurants.

  3. The beaches & the reef

    Pristine beaches with turtle nesting sites, and boats out to Lady Musgrave and Lady Elliot islands.

  4. Deepwater & Eurimbula

    National parks on either side of us.
