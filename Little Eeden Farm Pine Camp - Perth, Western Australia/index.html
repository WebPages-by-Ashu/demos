our little corner of Chittering

Ten acres of pine trees, a honey farm, and room to slow down

Little Eeden Farm Pine Camp is a small, off-grid campground on our working farm at Mooliabeenee, out in the Shire of Chittering.

The sites are set in among tall pines, so there's shade to park under and plenty of room to spread out. Bring the tent, the camper trailer or the van. You'll wake up to the sound of the bush and the farm animals, and on weekends The Hive Café is open for a feed and a free honey tasting.

It's a family-friendly place with a playground for the kids and a few simple camp rules to keep it pleasant for everyone.

— Arno & everyone at Little Eeden

Around camp

  • Toilets & showers

    Showers are on a three-minute rule. We're off-grid, so every drop counts.

  • Drinking water

    Potable water on site.

  • Fire pits

    Designated pits only, and only when there's no fire ban on.

  • Playground

    Somewhere for the kids to run off a bit of energy.

  • Farm animals

    Say hello, just don't chase them.

  • The Hive Café

    Saturdays, Sundays & public holidays, 11am–3pm.

  • Honey tastings

    Free, at the café. We're a honey farm and apiary too.

  • Dogs welcome

    On a lead and outdoors only, so not inside the café.

The camp rules

  • No smoking anywhere on the farm.
  • Fires go in the designated pits only. Never leave one unattended, and keep an adult on watch.
  • Generators 9am to 6pm only.
  • Quiet time from 10pm. Turn the music and voices down.
  • Save water & power. Keep showers to 3 minutes and switch off lights you're not using.
  • Dump point: the nearest one is at Bindoon Oval.
